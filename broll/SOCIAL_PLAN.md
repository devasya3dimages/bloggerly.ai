# bloggerly.ai — Motion B-roll Social Plan

55 static creatives → 55 looping 7-second motion clips, each in two cuts:

| Cut | Size | Use it on |
|---|---|---|
| `feed/` | 1080×1350 (4:5) | Instagram & Facebook feed, LinkedIn, X |
| `reels/` | 1080×1920 (9:16) | Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Stories |

Built from the clips themselves, not just the stills:

- **Frame 0 is the original poster.** The auto-picked cover is the ad, so the grid stays clean with no blank or mid-zoom covers.
- **Seamless loop.** Each clip ends on its first frame. Short loops get rewatched, and rewatches count as watch time, which the algorithms reward.
- **Headline and logo stay pinned** on the illustrated/photo posters while the scene moves underneath, so the hook stays readable for the whole clip. Full-bleed anime and horror art moves as one piece and gets its energy from effects instead (flicker, shake, glow).
- **9:16 safe zones.** The poster sits 210 px from the top, so the bloggerly.ai wordmark clears the TikTok/Reels caption and button area.
- **Silent track on purpose.** Add a trending sound in-app; trending audio gets more reach than baked-in music. Each file has a silent AAC track so every uploader accepts it.

## Motion language per series

| Series | Motion | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Original Concepts | Slow museum push, slow spin on top-down shots, snail pans left→right | Premium, calm, "gallery" pace |
| Flirty | Slow push + rise with a warm drifting light leak | Soft, romantic, sensual pacing |
| Horror | Creeping push, flicker drops, vignette, grain | Classic jump-scare grammar; flicker frames stop the scroll |
| Playful | Beat-synced bounce pops (120 BPM) and rise | Lines up with most trending audio at 120 BPM |
| Friends / sitcom | Bounce on group shots, *documentary crash-zoom* on reaction shots | Sitcom "reaction cut" energy |
| The Office | Crash-zoom + handheld wobble | The mockumentary camera *is* the joke |
| Stranger Things | Chasing Christmas lights, red glow pulses, flicker | Recognisable at a glance in the first second |
| Breaking Bad / Science | Desert heat-haze, lab pans, glow on the blue crystal draft | Matches the show's look |
| Attack on Titan / Anime | Tilt-up reveal on the titan, impact shake, crash-zooms | Anime opening-sequence energy |
| Lord of the Rings / Fantasy | Slow epic push, pan across the rope bridge, map spin | "Journey" pacing |

**Carousels.** Each series folder is a ready-made 4–7 slide Instagram/LinkedIn carousel ("The bloggerly.ai Stranger Things collection"). Use the feed cuts as video slides.

## Captions

The format: the headline is already the hook, so the caption *adds* to the joke and doesn't repeat it. Then one line of product value, then a soft CTA.

**Hashtag set** (pick 3–5, rotate): `#contentmarketing #blogging #bloggingtips #contentcreator #marketingmemes #writerslife #seo #aiwriting #contentstrategy #b2bmarketing`
Series-specific tags: add the show tag (e.g. `#strangerthings #breakingbad #friends #theoffice #attackontitan #lotr`) only on the matching parody.

## Before posting the parodies

The Friends, Office, Stranger Things, Breaking Bad and Attack on Titan posters use recognisable characters. Get legal/brand sign-off before running them as **paid** ads. Organic meme posts carry less risk, but platforms can still take them down.
